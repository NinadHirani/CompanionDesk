      Proactive reminders

      Off by default. Nothing is monitored and nothing fires until you both add a schedule and turn delivery on. Quiet hours defer a due reminder to the end of the window; they never drop it. Everything stays on this computer.

      Voice input

      Off by default. Voice runs entirely on this computer: audio exists only while you are recording, goes to the local whisper program, and is then discarded; nothing is stored and nothing is uploaded. The microphone is used only during an explicit Speak press, never at startup.

      Wake word

      Off by default. When you start it, CompanionDesk listens on this computer only for "Hey <your companion's name>" and opens a hands-free question without any button press. Nothing leaves this computer: audio is analyzed frame by frame in memory and discarded, nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded, and no network is involved. The microphone is used only while listening, the wake card below shows the live state (including when the microphone is open), and Stop closes the microphone immediately. The wake model is trained for your companion's exact name; changing the name stops wake listening until the model is retrained.

      Screen observation

      Off by default. Nothing looks at your screen until you pick a mode below and explicitly start it. Everything is processed on this computer only: nothing is stored and nothing is uploaded. The screen-capture permission is requested only when you act, never at startup. Background assist exists only on laptops and desktops; it never runs on Android.

      Provider status

        Checked on demand only: nothing polls, and Gemini's reachability is never probed on its own. Status comes from the local Ollama and this platform's credential storage.
